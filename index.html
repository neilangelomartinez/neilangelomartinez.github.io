About

I build ad systems that bring in leads, lift sales, and turn a profit, with a data analyst's eye for what's actually working.

I started as a data analyst for two years, then moved into media buying because I wanted to turn the numbers into decisions, not just reports. Two years in now, my main work is Meta Ads on Facebook and Instagram.
